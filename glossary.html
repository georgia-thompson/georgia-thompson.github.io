Glossary by Project

Texas Education Data

  • RELATIVE_WEEKLY_SALARY: A location's average weekly salary relative to the average salary in Texas.
    Location's Average Salary / Texas Average Salary
  • ECONOMIC_DENSITY: A location's number of businesses per employed person.
    Number of Establishments / Number of Employed People
  • ECONOMIC_ACTIVITY_INDEX: The distribution of the spending money to the location's businesses.
    ECONOMIC_DENSITY * RELATIVE_WEEKLY_SALARY
  • PART_RATE: The percentage of students in the graduation class taking the SAT.
  • EXNEES_MSKD: The estimated number of students taking the SAT score for a graduation class.
  • DISTRICT_TYPE_: Any of the features following "DISTRICT_TYPE_" are the NCES district types, which can be found here. For instance, DISTRICT_TYPE_Town-Remote is the "Town-Remote" NCES district type.
